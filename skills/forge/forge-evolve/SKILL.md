## Rules

- Identity evolution is a LAGGING indicator — moat dimensions are leading
- Run quarterly minimum — more frequent adds noise, less loses strategic perspective
- CEO self-assessment on identity must be HONEST — no aspirational scoring
- Three Laws check keeps FORGE grounded in reality
- Moat score without physical validation evidence = inflated — cross-check with HELIX dP/dt
