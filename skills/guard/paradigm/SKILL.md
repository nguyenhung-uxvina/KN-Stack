Conduct a formal paradigm challenge on a deeply held assumption or mental model.

Usage: /paradigm [assumption_to_challenge] OR provide details interactively.

1. If $ARGUMENTS provided, use as the assumption to challenge; otherwise ask:
   - What belief, assumption, or mental model do you want to challenge?
   - How long have you held this belief?
   - What decisions depend on this being true?
   - What would change if it were wrong?

2. Execute the paradigm challenge:

3. Present to user. WAIT for CEO decision — paradigm shifts are the highest-leverage intervention (Meadows Level 2).

RULES:
- This is the MOST POWERFUL and MOST DANGEROUS tool — changing paradigms changes everything downstream
- Quarterly use maximum — too frequent = instability, too rare = rigidity
- CEO must decide — AI cannot challenge paradigms it helped build (R3 risk)
- Always steel-man the alternative — weak alternatives don't test anything
