## Degrees of Novelty (P&B 3.1.1)

| Type | Mô tả | Ví dụ Workshop X | Risk |
|------|--------|-------------------|------|
| **Original** | New function, new principle | VN-USV-SS-001 (semi-sub USV) | HIGH |
| **Adaptive** | Known function, new arrangement | BB-01 LOMAH (piezo contact) | MEDIUM |
| **Variant** | Size/arrangement change only | VN-12.7MM-SIM (recoil variant) | LOW |

## Charter Checklist (from P&B 3.1 + Workshop X)

- [ ] Problem statement (1-2 sentences, solution-neutral)
- [ ] Novelty classification (Original/Adaptive/Variant)
- [ ] Strategic alignment (which capability gap?)
- [ ] Customer identification (who pays? who uses?)
- [ ] Known constraints (budget, timeline, standards)
- [ ] Triple Helix domains identified (Mech/Elec/AI)
- [ ] Similar products in portfolio (reuse potential)
- [ ] ACH assessment needed? (→ forge-shift)

## Vietnamese Defense Context

- Resource constraints → rigorous planning prevents waste
- Technology transfer → plan absorption strategy
- Dual-use potential → consider export from start
- Standards: TCVN, MIL-STD, STANAG requirements identified early
