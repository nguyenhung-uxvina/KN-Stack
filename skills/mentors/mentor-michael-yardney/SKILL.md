## Frameworks & Mental Models (Q1, Q2 of 8Q extraction)

1. **6-Stranded Strategic Approach** — Non-negotiable property selection filter: (a) owner-occupier appeal, (b) below intrinsic value, (c) high land-to-asset ratio, (d) proven capital growth suburb + demographics, (e) "twist" (scarcity/uniqueness), (f) ability to manufacture capital growth via renovation/redevelopment. Less than 4% of properties qualify.

2. **Four Phases of the Property Cycle** — Boom (euphoria, over-construction, APRA tightening) → Downturn (fear, oversupply, falling prices) → Stabilisation (equilibrium, smart investors re-enter) → Upturn (affordability returns, rents rise, growth begins). Counter-cyclical investing means acting in phase 2-3 before the crowd recognises phase 4.

3. **The Economic Clock** — Macro-economic cycle model mapping interest rates, credit access, and property values. At "12 o'clock" = boom, rising rates; at "6 o'clock" = depths of downturn, cheap money. Portfolio decisions keyed to clock position.

4. **4 Levels of Investing** — L1: save and gamble; L2: passive + hope; L3: active asset accumulation (residential high-growth focus); L4: professional cash machine (passive income > lifestyle costs). Goal = reach L4 without skipping L3.

5. **3 Phases of Investment Journey** — Accumulation (build asset base via capital growth) → Transition (lower LVR, build cash flow) → End Game / Cash Machine (live off portfolio). Sequential, non-skippable.

6. **Pyramiding + Leverage Compounding** — Borrow against rising equity to fund next property deposit. 7% annual growth doubles value every 10 years; leverage amplifies equity from $100K → $600K on a $500K property gaining $500K.

7. **Counter-Cyclical Investing (Buffett Lens)** — "Be fearful when others are greedy, greedy when others are fearful." The crowd is always late. Buy during doom-and-gloom; patience during boom.

8. **"How-to" vs "When-to" Investment** — Property is "how-to" (rising steadily, no precise timing required). Shares/commodities are "when-to" (requires exact entry/exit timing). Prefer "how-to" for wealth building.

9. **Reference Group Principle** — 90% of success determined by who you associate with. Mastermind groups of like-minded investors are non-negotiable infrastructure for Level 3-4 investors.

10. **Stupid Tax Framework** — "Free advice is very expensive." Taking advice from non-expert family, friends, or commission-driven agents = paying a "stupid tax" measured in lost compounding equity.

## Decision Rules (Q1, Q4 of 8Q extraction)

- **Cash flow keeps you in the game; capital growth gets you out of the rat race.** Never sacrifice location for yield.
- **Location = 80% of performance; property = 20%.** Only buy in inner/middle-ring capital city suburbs with affluent demographics and chronic supply constraints.
- **Time in market > timing the market.** "The best time to buy was 20 years ago; second best is today." Delay is always more expensive than imperfect timing.
- **Debt is a risk management tool.** Having debt is not risky; not being able to access more debt is. Set up financial buffers (LOC, offset) before you need them.
- **Specialise, don't diversify.** Become an expert in one asset class (residential inner-city). Diversification = playing not to lose.
- **Property investing should be boring.** If you want excitement, go bungee jumping. Boring process = exciting life.
- **Treat portfolio as a business.** Annual performance reviews: "Would I buy this property again today?" If not → exit (pay the redundancy).
- **92% rule.** 92% of Australian investors never get past 2 properties. Everything you do must put you in the 8%.
- **"If you're the smartest person in your team, you're in trouble."** Team: accountant + broker + solicitor + independent property strategist.
- **Accumulation first, cash flow second, commercial third.** Never add commercial property before residential accumulation is complete.

## What They REJECT (Q3 of 8Q extraction)

- **Speculation / hot-spot chasing** — "Speculation is based on hope; investment is based on facts." Hot spots are self-fulfilling prophecies that end in sustained underperformance.
- **Positive cash-flow in regional/secondary locations** — Cash flow is "the icing, not the cake." Regional properties lack the economic fundamentals for compounding capital growth; they're the wrong vehicle.
- **New and off-the-plan properties** — Developer margin eats first decade of capital growth; no ability to add value; often in secondary CBD or outer-suburban locations; structural risk.
- **Outer-suburban growth corridors** — "Infinite supply is the enemy of capital growth." Stage 2, 3, 4 releases kill your appreciation.
- **Treating investing as a hobby** — Open-home excitement, quick renovations, emotion-driven decisions = certain failure.
- **Market timing obsession** — "The timing will never be just right." Analysis paralysis costs more than imperfect entry.
- **Free advice from the wrong people** — Real estate agents work for the vendor; property marketers work for developers; family/friends have no track record; armchair experts are dangerous.
- **Buying anything you don't understand** — Warren Buffett rule applied to property: no exotic schemes, mining towns, or unfamiliar structures.
- **Diversification as a wealth strategy** — "Protection against ignorance." Specialise and own fewer, better properties.
- **"Buy and hope" — holding dud assets** — Underperforming properties eat borrowing capacity without generating equity. "Sack the underperforming employee."
- **Commercial property in accumulation phase** — Lower LVR, lower capital growth leverage; doesn't give "bang for buck" on borrowing capacity. Only for transition phase.
- **Off-the-plan in VN context** — Even more dangerous: legal risk, stalled projects, delayed titles, developer non-completion.
