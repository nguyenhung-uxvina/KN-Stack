## Frameworks & Mental Models (Q1, Q2 of 8Q extraction)

1. **Kill Chain** — Warfare = sequential process of understand → decide → act. Goal: close this chain at greater speed and scale than adversaries. Not about specific platforms — about the *process* of decision advantage. Every military system is evaluated by how well it contributes to closing the kill chain. [Foreign Affairs 2019; Reimagining 2024; Hidden Forces 2023]

2. **Kill Web** — Kill chain operates as a distributed, decentralized network ("web"), not a linear link. Sensors, decision-makers, and effectors can be any node; redundancy = resilience against systems destruction. Applies same logic as kill chain but architecturally distributed. [Reimagining 2024; Hudson 2020]

3. **Systems Destruction Warfare (China's Doctrine)** — China's military strategy is NOT to match the US platform-for-platform. It is to systematically destroy the *underlying battle network* — forward bases, space assets, electromagnetic spectrum access, C2 communications, logistics — rendering US exquisite platforms deaf, dumb, and blind before engagement. This is qualitatively and quantitatively superior to Russian approach. [Reimagining 2024; Foreign Affairs 2019]

4. **Porcupine Strategy / Insurgent Defense** — For smaller nations or A2/AD posture: don't try to project power; play the "insurgent" against power-projecting adversary. Use ubiquitous sensors + low-cost precision strike + anti-access/area-denial to make power projection prohibitively costly. Ukraine validates this. Technology democratizes: small nations can now punch enormously above weight. [Reimagining 2024; ChinaTalk 2025]

5. **Human Command, Machine Control** — Reject binary "killer robots" vs. total human control. Framework: humans *command* (set intent, parameters, make key moral decisions — especially classifying whether an object is a legitimate military target); machines *control* (execute tactical tasks at speed and scale, including defensive autonomous responses). Defensive operations → delegate much more to machines (speed required, casualties of inaction high). Offensive → more human oversight. [Hudson 2020; ChinaTalk 2025; Foreign Affairs 2019]

6. **Consumable Military / Precise Mass** — Historically: mass (dumb, cheap) OR precision (smart, expensive). Modern convergence resolves false choice: low-cost autonomous systems give *both* mass and precision. Design systems as consumables: buy in quantity, replace every 2-3 years, commercial supply chains, simple assembly (IKEA furniture, not fighter jets). Redesigning from the beginning for mass-producibility is the only way to scale. [ChinaTalk 2025; Tech Summit 2025; Reimagining 2024]

7. **Outcome-Based Procurement (Sabermetrics)** — Defense acquisition measures bureaucratic inputs (PowerPoint requirements, cost certainty, compliance). Must shift to measuring *operational outcomes*: real-world competitions every 1-2 years, evaluated on cost/range/effectiveness in field conditions. Winner gets production contract; competition runs again in 2 years. Creates market incentives. "We should measure team outcomes not player inputs." [Hudson 2020; Reimagining 2024]

8. **Defense Without Dominance** — US no longer has assured operational dominance; 10 years of wargames against China = US has nearly perfect loss record. Reframe: not "how do we restore dominance" but "how do we deny adversary dominance while building sufficient deterrence and denial capability." [Foreign Affairs 2019; NDU Press 2021; Strategy Bridge 2021]

9. **Valley of Death Bridge (SBIR Pathway)** — "Never been easier to get a small contract; never been harder to cross to production." SBIR Phase 1→2→3 legally allows sole-source production after competitive Phase 1-2 proving. The authorities exist; the barrier is political will and educated leadership. Reform is a cop-out — courageous use of existing authority is the answer. [Tech Summit 2025; Reimagining 2024]

10. **Independent Conviction** — Defense tech company must develop its own vision of right answers; cannot wait for government requirements. Industry's role: set the predicate (demonstrate capability) → government's role: adopt new ways of operating that this capability enables. Government cannot imagine what it hasn't seen. Division of labor: industry imagines + proves; government operates + adapts. [ChinaTalk 2025]

## Decision Rules (Q1, Q4 of 8Q extraction)

- **Design for mass-producibility from day 1** — no downstream fix for upstream design choices; if system is designed to be irreplaceable, no amount of money fixes it later; commercial supply chains only from requirements phase
- **Measure outcomes not inputs** — run real-world field competitions annually; metric is operational effectiveness (cost/range/lethality), not compliance/schedule/PowerPoint
- **Human command, machine control** — human classifies target as legitimate; machine executes; defensive high-speed operations → delegate more; offensive target selection → human mandatory
- **Defensive operations get maximum autonomy** — speed of inbound threat often requires autonomous response; inaction/delay = friendly casualties; counter-drone, CWIS, point defense → AI autonomy justified
- **Use SBIR Phase 1→2→3 pathway** — Phase 3 sole-source to winner is the *law*; authorities exist; demand leaders who will use them
- **Competition every 2-3 years, not tenure** — programs of record should be competed regularly; winner today must re-compete; losers get another shot; prevents stagnation
- **Commercial supply chains exclusively** — eliminate defense-unique materials, specs, specialized workforce that doesn't scale; design for automotive/commercial manufacturing workforce
- **Small nations play porcupine, not power projection** — invest in ubiquitous sensors + low-cost precision strike + A2/AD; this strategy is affordable and increasingly effective
- **Industry sets predicate, government adapts** — best ideas for operational change come from bottom up in government; industry provides technological pre-conditions; senior leader "top cover" required to implement
- **Seriousness = actions, not words** — judged by programs started, money moved, capabilities fielded; process reform = cop-out; courage is the scarce resource

## What Brose REJECTS (Q3 of 8Q extraction)

- **Exquisite legacy platforms** — small numbers, high cost, irreplaceable, maintained 30-40 years; exact opposite of what modern warfare requires
- **"Short war" assumption** — ahistorical; every US war lasted far longer than planned; Ukraine/Gaza validates this; design and stockpile accordingly
- **Future requirements prediction** — cannot write requirements for systems needed in 2032; technology changes too fast; outcome-based competitions replace predictive requirements
- **Programs of record as tenure** — once awarded, immune to disruption; closes door on new capabilities and better solutions
- **Defense-unique supply chains** — unscalable materials, specialized workforce that takes years to train; makes mass production structurally impossible
- **Nationalizing prime contractors** — "catastrophically bad right now"; creates conflicts of interest; undermines competitive renaissance from new defense tech companies
- **Binary "killer robots" debate** — paralyzing policy debate that prevents adoption of beneficial autonomy; existing C2 law + framework already provides answers
- **Lack of seriousness** — "Are we serious or not?" — hand-waving vulnerabilities, using process reform as excuse for inaction; limited only by imagination and courage, not authorities or money
- **Blockbuster upgrading Blockbuster** — incremental improvements to legacy platforms just build bigger targets; "Doubling down on status quo is exactly what our competitors want"

VN-CUAS-001 Boyd FCS autonomous defensive layer = aligned with Brose's human command/machine control distinction.
