## Bio

Dan Sullivan is the founder of Strategic Coach (est. 1974, with Babs Smith) — one of the world's longest-running and most influential entrepreneur coaching programs, with 20,000+ entrepreneurs coached over 50 years. Born in 1944, Sullivan began his career in advertising and marketing before discovering his calling: helping entrepreneurs achieve extraordinary results by designing their lives and businesses around their Unique Ability. His frameworks are empirically derived from observing what actually works across thousands of entrepreneurs — not from academic theory.

His prolific collaboration with psychologist Dr. Benjamin Hardy produced three major books (2020-2023): *Who Not How*, *The Gap and The Gain*, and *10x Is Easier Than 2x* — all reaching millions of readers globally. His Strategic Coach program operates at three levels (Foundation, 10x Ambition, Free Zone) and has participants who remain in the program for 10-25+ years.

## Frameworks & Mental Models (Q1, Q2)

1. **The 4 Freedoms** — Meta-goal of all Sullivan frameworks. All entrepreneurial aspiration reduces to: Freedom of Time (you own your schedule), Freedom of Money (income decoupled from hours), Freedom of Relationship (you choose every relationship), Freedom of Purpose (work aligned with Unique Ability). Every tactical framework below is a means to one or more of these. [Strategic Coach Core Philosophy]

2. **Unique Ability** — The specific activities where you demonstrate: superior skill, passion/love, energy generation (gives energy rather than depletes it), and creates greatest value for others. Four zones: Zone 1 (Unique Ability — 80% target), Zone 2 (Excellent — biggest trap), Zone 3 (Competent — delegate first), Zone 4 (Incompetent — eliminate immediately). [Unique Ability, w/ Nomura]

3. **10x Is Easier Than 2x** — 10x growth requires eliminating 80% of current activities and focusing on the 20% that creates genuine 10x value. 2x thinking keeps the 80% noise. 10x thinking is a filter, not just a goal — it makes obvious what to say no to. When things get more complex, you're on a 2x path. [10x Is Easier Than 2x, 2023]

4. **Who Not How** — When facing any challenge, don't ask "How do I do this?" Ask "Who can do this?" "How" thinking traps you in execution, makes you the bottleneck, and shrinks your vision to what you personally can execute. "Who" thinking opens you to leverage. Wanting to know "how" is really about control. [Who Not How, 2020]

5. **The Gap and The Gain** — Two ways to measure progress: The Gap (measuring against your ideal — creates chronic dissatisfaction, even during success) vs The Gain (measuring backward against where you started — creates confidence, resilience, gratitude). High achievers are uniquely susceptible to Gap thinking. The antidote: end every day/quarter with "What did I gain?" [The Gap and The Gain, 2021]

6. **The 4 C's Formula** — Growth follows a fixed sequence: Commitment → Courage → Capability → Confidence. You must commit BEFORE you have capability. Commitment creates the pressure that develops capability. Confidence is the result of completing cycles, not the prerequisite for starting them. The cycle is infinite — each new capability enables a bigger commitment. [The 4 C's Formula]

7. **The Entrepreneurial Time System** — Three day types: Free Days (24h of complete disconnection — zero work; 150+/year target; makes Focus Days 3-5x more productive), Focus Days (80%+ in Unique Ability; pre-planned, protected from operational noise), Buffer Days (preparation, meetings, clearing — enables Focus Days to stay pure). [The Entrepreneurial Time System]

8. **Always Be the Buyer** — In every interaction, you are either Buyer (sets standards) or Seller (adapts to them). Buyers attract; Sellers chase. Your price is a signal of your standards — never lower it under pressure. Premium pricing repels the wrong clients and attracts serious ones. Applies to: client selection, hiring, partnerships, pricing. [Always Be the Buyer]

9. **The Self-Managing Company** — Goal: company operates without entrepreneur's daily involvement. Requires 3 transformations: Operator → Communicator, Decision-Maker → Standard-Setter, Bottleneck → Multiplier. Key hire: Manager of Everything (MOE) whose Unique Ability is managing complexity. Primary CEO tool: the Impact Filter (purpose, importance, ideal outcome, best/worst result, success criteria). [The Self-Managing Company, 2020]

10. **The 8 Patterns of Consistent Earners** — Empirically derived: (1) Commit before having capability, (2) Focus on what you control, (3) Always learning from every experience, (4) Only work with positive people, (5) Think about the future, not the past, (6) Grateful for everything, (7) Refer to values, (8) Confidence as most important asset. [No Matter What]

11. **The Strategy Circle** — 4-question framework for any challenge: (1) Where are you now? (2) Where do you want to be? (3) What obstacles stand in the way? (4) What strategy best overcomes those obstacles? Forces naming the obstacles before planning — prevents wishful-thinking strategy. [The Strategy Circle]

12. **Multiplication vs Addition Leadership** — Addition leadership: 1+1=2 (each new hire adds linearly). Multiplication leadership: 1+1=5 (each new hire placed in their Unique Ability zone multiplies others' output). Requires: Unique Ability-based hiring, real delegation, strong culture. [Dan Sullivan on Team Building]

13. **The Connection Economy** — Most durable entrepreneurial asset: community of relationships where you're the trusted source of direction and value. Built by: consistent genuine value delivery, Always Be the Buyer in every relationship, forward-focused communication, deep appreciation of your network. [Dan Sullivan on Money]

## Decision Rules (Q1, Q4)

- **10x Test**: Before any new project, commitment, or relationship — "Does this move me toward 10x or merely 2x?"
- **Unique Ability Filter**: Before taking on any task — "Is this in my Unique Ability or am I settling for Excellent?"
- **Who Before How**: When facing any challenge — "Who can do this?" not "How do I do this?"
- **Buyer or Seller?**: Before any client meeting — "Am I entering as Buyer (I will qualify this person) or Seller (I will convince this person)?"
- **Gain Measurement**: End every day, week, 90 days with "What did I gain?" BEFORE setting next goals
- **Price signal**: Never lower price under pressure. "Your price is a message about your value."
- **Commitment rule**: "A goal you can easily achieve doesn't change you. Only a commitment that scares you produces transformation."
- **Complexity warning**: When anything is getting more complex, stop — it's a sign of 2x thinking or unclear priorities
- **Free Day standard**: 24h = zero work. Never check email, phone, or business during Free Days
- **Hiring filter**: "Does this person have their Unique Ability in the zone I need?" — not "Are they competent?"
- **Pricing review**: Annual check — "Have my results improved? My price should reflect this."
- **Control inventory**: What can I control (preparation, standards, relationships, commitments) vs cannot (market, circumstances)? Invest only in the first.
- **Directional vs operational risk**: Spend almost no time evaluating "will this tactic work?" — spend significant time on "is this the right strategic direction?"

## What Dan Sullivan Rejects (Q3)

- **Hard work / hustle culture**: "Hard work in your Unique Ability zone is not hard — it's energizing. Hard work outside your Unique Ability zone destroys you." Rejects "rise at 5am, grind forever" culture as a recipe for burnout.
- **Fixing weaknesses**: "You will never be better than mediocre in your weakness area." Your weakness is someone else's Unique Ability — invest $1 there and get marginal returns. Invest in Unique Ability and get compound returns.
- **"How" addiction and self-sufficiency**: Heroic solo operator is not a valid business model. "Self-sufficiency is not a virtue in entrepreneurship."
- **Discounting and standards compromise**: "Any client is better than no client" is a trap. Undercharging is cowardly, not humble.
- **Comparison with others**: "You are your only fair competitor." Benchmark against your own prior year, not competitors or industry standards.
- **Complexity as sophistication**: "If you can't explain your strategy on a napkin, you don't have a strategy — you have a mess."
- **Incremental goal-setting**: Safe goals don't trigger the 4 C's cycle. The commitment must exceed current capability.
- **Business-life separation**: Business is an expression of your life, not separate from it. Goal is alignment, not balance.
- **Retirement for entrepreneurs**: "Retirement is the goal of someone who doesn't like their work." If work is Unique Ability, you never want to stop.
- **Gap thinking**: Measuring against the ideal creates chronic dissatisfaction, fragile confidence, and poor decisions.
- **Victim narrative**: "I cannot control the market. I can control my preparation, my standards, my relationships, and my commitment."
