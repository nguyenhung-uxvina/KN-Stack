## Frameworks & Mental Models (Q1, Q2 of 8Q extraction)

1. **Iterative Deployment Speed (3-Week Cycle)** — 80% solution deployed in 3 weeks > 100% solution in 3 years. Technology change cycle on the battlefield does not exceed 3 months; waiting for perfection means delivering obsolete technology. Ukraine scaled from concept to combat in weeks, not years. [KSE Institute 2024/2025; Brave1 Wikipedia]

2. **Absolute EW Resilience Baseline** — Always assume GPS is spoofed, RF communications are jammed, internet is absent. Design every AI inference system, drone navigation, and FCS from this baseline. Cloud APIs are not an option in contested environments. [CSIS/CSET 2025; VGI-9/The Fourth Law via CSIS]

3. **Asymmetric Cost-Exchange Economics** — Interceptor cost must be exponentially lower than target cost. SAMs ($400K-1M) vs. Shaheds ($35K) = national bankruptcy. Interceptor drones ($800) vs. reconnaissance UAVs = 1:10 cost ratio advantage. [KSE Institute 2025]

4. **Passive Sensor Fusion Matrix** — Detection must be passive, layered, decentralized: acoustic network (Zvook: $500/station, 4.8km range, 1.6% false positive) → acoustic triggers thermal slew → optical confirmation → Common Operating Picture (Delta). Active radar = "Beacon of Death" (invites anti-radiation strike). [CSIS 2025; Ukraine EW Wikipedia]

5. **Decoupled Autonomy Moat** — Hardware is attritable COTS; the strategic moat is the encrypted edge-AI software module ($50-100, soap-sized). Enemies replicate hardware in weeks; encrypted AI algorithms take months to reverse-engineer. [The Fourth Law/ZIR System via CSIS; KSE 2024]

6. **UTM Paradox: Deconfliction Under Fire** — In conflict, UTM is not civilian traffic management — it is instantaneous deconfliction to prevent drone fratricide. Friendly EW frequently jams friendly drones. Civilian and military airspace/maritime domains must be separated but information-shared. [CSIS CSET NATO Forum; CSIS autonomous warfare]

7. **Small Model / Edge Inference Paradigm** — Specialized small AI models (CNN/RNN) trained on curated combat datasets (Brave1 Dataroom) run on Jetson Orin-class hardware. ATR range: 1-2km lock-on. Massive cloud-dependent AI models fail in EW environments. [CSIS AI autonomous warfare; Brave1 Dataroom]

8. **Operator-Engineer Symbiosis** — End-user is co-developer. Embed R&D engineers directly at frontline units for ultra-low-latency feedback. Software updated the morning after operator complaints. Quantum Systems model: 3 hardware revisions within 12 months from field feedback. [KSE 2024 Quantum Systems case study]

## Decision Rules (Q1, Q4 of 8Q extraction)

- **Detection hierarchy: acoustic → thermal slew-to-cue → optical confirmation.** Never rely on single sensor. Passive acoustic triggers target slew; thermal confirms; optical engages. [Zvook / CSIS 2025]
- **ATR must run 100% offline on edge hardware.** If your system calls a cloud API for target recognition, it will fail under EW. Jetson Orin or equivalent is the minimum compute platform. [CSIS; VGI-9]
- **90-day obsolescence window.** If your development cycle > 90 days, you are building obsolete tech. Sprint every 3 weeks, deploy to field, collect data, push update. [KSE 2024]
- **Cost-exchange hierarchy: EW jamming ($100/engagement) → interceptor drone ($800) → kinetic (last resort).** Never fire expensive weapons at cheap mass threats. [KSE 2025]
- **Turn off active radar; use acoustic networks as primary detection.** Active radar = target signature for anti-radiation strike. Default to passive Zvook-type acoustic arrays. [IEEE Spectrum; CSIS]
- **Encrypt AI software, treat hardware as disposable.** Adversary can copy airframe in weeks. Encrypted ATR module protects your strategic moat even if drone is captured. [CSIS; The Fourth Law]
- **Fly-before-you-buy under active EW conditions.** Test under military-grade RF jamming and GPS spoofing. Lab performance is irrelevant. [TRADOC 2024; KSE 2025]
- **Embed one engineer at the operational unit.** R&D cannot write effective field code from a headquarters office. Engineer must experience the environment. [KSE 2024]
- **Frequency-hop or use fiber.** No single static RF channel; autonomous SNR monitoring + cruise control mode when jammed. Fiber-optic = 100% EW immunity at short range. [VGI; Ukraine EW Wikipedia]
- **Data is your strategic weapon.** Capture every maritime/surveillance frame as future AI training data. Build a "platform dataroom" for continuous ATR model improvement. [Brave1 Dataroom; CSIS]

## What They REJECT (Q3 of 8Q extraction)

- **MIL-SPEC monolith** — Multi-year development cycles that deliver obsolete technology. "We wait for the perfect system while the imperfect threat exists today." [TRADOC 2024; CSIS]
- **Cloud-tethered AI** — Any ATR or navigation system requiring internet connectivity. Under EW, cloud = zero. [CSIS AI warfare; VGI-9]
- **Static radio frequencies** — Drones and FCS on fixed RF bands are instantly jammed. Skydio X2 failure in 2022 is the canonical case study. [CSIS Skydio case study]
- **Active radar as primary sensor** — "Active radar is emitting signals, so if you are using active radars, you are target No. 1 on the front line." [IEEE Spectrum/Falcons; CSIS]
- **SAMs vs. cheap drones (inverted cost-exchange)** — Firing $400K+ missiles at $500-35K threats mathematically exhausts defense budgets. [KSE 2025]
- **Single-sensor detection** — Optical alone: blinded by fog/smoke/camouflage. Acoustic alone: too many false positives. Radar alone: emission signature. Multi-sensor fusion mandatory. [CSIS 2025]
- **GPS dependency** — Any system whose primary navigation or detection requires uncontested GNSS will fail. VIO and INS are mandatory fallbacks. [CSIS; Ukraine EW Wikipedia]
- **Broad-spectrum heavy jamming as sole defense** — Causes drone fratricide (friendly EW jams friendly drones). Decentralized directional trench-level EW bubbles required. [CSIS CSET; Ukraine EW Wikipedia]
- **Bespoke expensive hardware** — Proprietary drone frames are not the moat. COTS attritable hardware + proprietary encrypted software is the correct architecture. [KSE 2024; The Fourth Law]
